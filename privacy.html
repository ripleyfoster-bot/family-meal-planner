Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Family Meal Planner is a personal household tool. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data stored on your device

All household data — member names, food preferences, meal plans, recipes, and shopping lists — is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server except as described below.

Data sent to Anthropic

When you ask the app to suggest recipes or categorise a meal, your request (including the text you type, meal slot context, and household preference summary) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate suggestions. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this data: anthropic.com/privacy.

We do not send personally identifiable information to Anthropic. Member names and specific food notes are included only to personalise meal suggestions and are not stored by us.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not use cookies or tracking
  • We do not create accounts or store any data on our servers
  • We do not share your data with any third party other than Anthropic (for AI suggestions)

Cookbook photos

If you photograph a cookbook page, the image is sent to Anthropic's API for text extraction. The image is not stored by us after the recipe is extracted. Anthropic's data handling policies apply.

Children's data

This app is designed for household use and may contain names or preferences of household members including children. All data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Your rights

You can delete all app data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or uninstalling the app from your home screen.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the person who set up your household's app.
